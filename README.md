## Data source and provenance

The underlying judicial data originates from French judicial open data produced by the **Cour de cassation** and distributed through **Judilibre**.

The source data is distributed under the **Licence Ouverte / Open Licence 2.0 (Etalab)**, subject to its applicable terms.

Legal Data France adds data-engineering work including:

- preparation and structuring
- conservative cleaning and normalization
- cross-domain deduplication
- synchronized AI-oriented formats
- Q&A generation
- instruction/SFT formatting
- RAG chunk preparation
- heuristic privacy-risk controls
- release/quarantine filtering
- integrity checks
- SHA-256 hashes
- provenance and release manifests

---

## Quality and privacy

The privacy controls used in this project are **technical and heuristic**.

They are not a legal certification that the dataset is fully anonymous or free from personal data.

An additional **80 decisions were excluded from the distributed release** following the current automated control process.

The dataset is intended for data, research, search and AI development workflows.

It does **not** provide legal advice.
